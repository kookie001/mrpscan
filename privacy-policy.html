Privacy Policy

MRP Scan

Effective Date: 10 August 2026 Last Updated: 10 August 2026

MRP Scan is operated by Amitaash IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Amitaash", "we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the MRP Scan mobile application, website, services, and related features (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or using MRP Scan, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. About MRP Scan

MRP Scan is a business-focused jewellery management and pricing application intended for jewellery businesses and their authorised users.

The Service may provide functionality including:

  • Jewellery tag scanning and OCR
  • AI-assisted jewellery information extraction
  • Gold-rate and pricing information
  • Jewellery pricing and MRP calculation
  • Business and employee management
  • Credit/wallet-based scan usage
  • Application licensing and subscriptions
  • Credit recharge and payment functionality
  • Business configuration and pricing settings

2. Information We Collect

MRP Scan is designed primarily around mobile-number-based authentication.

We may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Mobile Number

Your mobile number may be collected when you:

  • Register for an account
  • Log in
  • Verify your identity using OTP
  • Access business functionality
  • Create or manage an employee account

The mobile number may be used as your primary account identifier.

MRP Scan does not require an email address as the primary identifier for application authentication.

2.2 Account and Business Information

Depending on the features used, we may collect information such as:

  • Name of the business or organisation
  • Business configuration
  • User role
  • Employee information
  • Application settings
  • Pricing configuration
  • Gold-rate configuration
  • Other information voluntarily entered into the application

2.3 Jewellery Tag Images

When you use the scanner, you may upload or capture photographs of jewellery tags.

These images may contain information including:

  • Gross weight
  • Net weight
  • Gold purity
  • Diamond weight
  • Diamond pieces
  • Diamond shape
  • Diamond colour
  • Diamond clarity
  • Colour-stone information
  • Packet codes
  • Product or stock identifiers
  • Other information printed on jewellery tags

These images are processed to provide the jewellery scanning and information-extraction functionality of MRP Scan.

2.4 Extracted Jewellery Information

The Service may process information extracted from jewellery tag images, including:

  • Weight information
  • Purity
  • Diamond information
  • Colour-stone information
  • Packet codes
  • Product identifiers
  • Other recognised tag information

Automated extraction may not always be accurate. Users should review extracted information before relying upon it for commercial, pricing, inventory, or other business decisions.

2.5 Usage and Technical Information

We may collect technical and operational information such as:

  • Scan activity
  • Scan timestamps
  • Application events
  • Authentication events
  • Error logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device/application information where technically required
  • Credit usage
  • Subscription/license status
  • Payment transaction status

This information helps us operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

3. OTP and Authentication

MRP Scan uses mobile-number-based authentication and OTP functionality.

When you request an OTP, your mobile number and relevant authentication information may be transmitted to an SMS/OTP service provider for delivery and verification.

OTP information is used for authentication and account-security purposes.

You should never disclose an OTP to another person.

MRP Scan will not intentionally request your OTP for purposes unrelated to authentication or account security.

4. Jewellery Images and AI/OCR Processing

MRP Scan may use OCR and artificial intelligence technologies to process jewellery tag images.

When you submit an image for scanning:

  1. The image is received by the MRP Scan service.
  2. The image may be transmitted to an AI/OCR processing service.
  3. Visible information from the jewellery tag is analysed.
  4. Relevant information is extracted into structured fields.
  5. The extracted information is returned to the application.
  6. The information may be used for pricing, review, and related application functionality.

The AI/OCR system is designed to identify information visible in the submitted image.

It should not be treated as a substitute for human verification.

MRP Scan does not guarantee that automated extraction will always be accurate.
